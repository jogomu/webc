# The Lamentations of Jeremiah

*[World English Bible (Catholic)](README.md) — Text version: signed by eBible.org 2026-07-24 (pulled 2026-07-26).*

## Lamentations 1

<sup>1</sup> How the city sits solitary,

&emsp;that was full of people!  
She has become as a widow,  
&emsp;who was great among the nations!  
She who was a princess among the provinces  
&emsp;has become a slave!

<sup>2</sup> She weeps bitterly in the night.  
&emsp;Her tears are on her cheeks.  
Among all her lovers  
&emsp;she has no one to comfort her.  
All her friends have dealt treacherously with her.  
&emsp;They have become her enemies.

<sup>3</sup> Judah has gone into captivity because of affliction  
&emsp;and because of great servitude.  
She dwells among the nations.  
&emsp;She finds no rest.  
&emsp;All her persecutors overtook her in her distress.

<sup>4</sup> The roads to Zion mourn,  
&emsp;because no one comes to the solemn assembly.  
All her gates are desolate.  
&emsp;Her priests sigh.  
Her virgins are afflicted,  
&emsp;and she herself is in bitterness.

<sup>5</sup> Her adversaries have become the head.  
&emsp;Her enemies prosper;  
for the LORD[^1] has afflicted her for the multitude of her transgressions.  
&emsp;Her young children have gone into captivity before the adversary.

<sup>6</sup> All majesty has departed from the daughter of Zion.  
&emsp;Her princes have become like deer that find no pasture.  
&emsp;They have gone without strength before the pursuer.

<sup>7</sup> Jerusalem remembers in the days of her affliction and of her miseries  
&emsp;all her pleasant things that were from the days of old;  
when her people fell into the hand of the adversary,  
&emsp;and no one helped her.  
The adversaries saw her.  
&emsp;They mocked at her desolations.

<sup>8</sup> Jerusalem has grievously sinned.  
&emsp;Therefore she has become unclean.  
All who honored her despise her,  
&emsp;because they have seen her nakedness.  
&emsp;Yes, she sighs and turns backward.

<sup>9</sup> Her filthiness was in her skirts.  
&emsp;She didn’t remember her latter end.  
Therefore she has come down astoundingly.  
&emsp;She has no comforter.  
“See, LORD, my affliction;  
&emsp;for the enemy has magnified himself.”

<sup>10</sup> The adversary has spread out his hand on all her pleasant things;  
&emsp;for she has seen that the nations have entered into her sanctuary,  
&emsp;concerning whom you commanded that they should not enter into your assembly.

<sup>11</sup> All her people sigh.  
&emsp;They seek bread.  
&emsp;They have given their pleasant things for food to refresh their soul.  
“Look, LORD, and see,  
&emsp;for I have become despised.”

<sup>12</sup> “Is it nothing to you, all you who pass by?  
&emsp;Look, and see if there is any sorrow like my sorrow,  
&emsp;which is brought on me,  
&emsp;with which the LORD has afflicted me in the day of his fierce anger.

<sup>13</sup> “From on high has he sent fire into my bones,  
&emsp;and it prevails against them.  
He has spread a net for my feet.  
&emsp;He has turned me back.  
&emsp;He has made me desolate and I faint all day long.

<sup>14</sup> “The yoke of my transgressions is bound by his hand.  
&emsp;They are knit together.  
&emsp;They have come up on my neck.  
&emsp;He made my strength fail.  
The Lord[^2] has delivered me into their hands,  
&emsp;against whom I am not able to stand.

<sup>15</sup> “The Lord has set at nothing all my mighty men within me.  
&emsp;He has called a solemn assembly against me to crush my young men.  
&emsp;The Lord has trodden the virgin daughter of Judah as in a wine press.

<sup>16</sup> “For these things I weep.  
&emsp;My eye, my eye runs down with water,  
&emsp;because the comforter who should refresh my soul is far from me.  
My children are desolate,  
&emsp;because the enemy has prevailed.”

<sup>17</sup> Zion spreads out her hands.  
&emsp;There is no one to comfort her.  
The LORD has commanded concerning Jacob,  
&emsp;that those who are around him should be his adversaries.  
&emsp;Jerusalem is among them as an unclean thing.

<sup>18</sup> “The LORD is righteous,  
&emsp;for I have rebelled against his commandment.  
Please hear all you peoples,  
&emsp;and see my sorrow.  
&emsp;My virgins and my young men have gone into captivity.

<sup>19</sup> “I called for my lovers,  
&emsp;but they deceived me.  
My priests and my elders gave up the spirit in the city,  
&emsp;while they sought food for themselves to refresh their souls.

<sup>20</sup> “Look, LORD; for I am in distress.  
&emsp;My heart is troubled.  
My heart turns over within me,  
&emsp;for I have grievously rebelled.  
Abroad, the sword bereaves.  
&emsp;At home, it is like death.

<sup>21</sup> “They have heard that I sigh.  
&emsp;There is no one to comfort me.  
All my enemies have heard of my trouble.  
&emsp;They are glad that you have done it.  
You will bring the day that you have proclaimed,  
&emsp;and they will be like me.

<sup>22</sup> “Let all their wickedness come before you.  
&emsp;Do to them as you have done to me for all my transgressions.  
For my sighs are many,  
&emsp;and my heart is faint.

## Lamentations 2

<sup>1</sup> How has the Lord covered the daughter of Zion with a cloud in his anger!

&emsp;He has cast the beauty of Israel down from heaven to the earth,  
&emsp;and hasn’t remembered his footstool in the day of his anger.

<sup>2</sup> The Lord has swallowed up all the dwellings of Jacob  
&emsp;without pity.  
He has thrown down in his wrath the strongholds of the daughter of Judah.  
&emsp;He has brought them down to the ground.  
&emsp;He has profaned the kingdom and its princes.

<sup>3</sup> He has cut off all the horn of Israel in fierce anger.  
&emsp;He has drawn back his right hand from before the enemy.  
He has burned up Jacob like a flaming fire,  
&emsp;which devours all around.

<sup>4</sup> He has bent his bow like an enemy.  
&emsp;He has stood with his right hand as an adversary.  
He has killed all that were pleasant to the eye.  
&emsp;In the tent of the daughter of Zion, he has poured out his wrath like fire.

<sup>5</sup> The Lord has become as an enemy.  
&emsp;He has swallowed up Israel.  
He has swallowed up all her palaces.  
&emsp;He has destroyed his strongholds.  
&emsp;He has multiplied mourning and lamentation in the daughter of Judah.

<sup>6</sup> He has violently taken away his tabernacle,  
&emsp;as if it were a garden.  
He has destroyed his place of assembly.  
&emsp;The LORD has caused solemn assembly and Sabbath to be forgotten in Zion.  
&emsp;In the indignation of his anger, he has despised the king and the priest.

<sup>7</sup> The Lord has cast off his altar.  
&emsp;He has abhorred his sanctuary.  
He has given the walls of her palaces into the hand of the enemy.  
&emsp;They have made a noise in the LORD’s house,  
&emsp;as in the day of a solemn assembly.

<sup>8</sup> The LORD has purposed to destroy the wall of the daughter of Zion.  
&emsp;He has stretched out the line.  
&emsp;He has not withdrawn his hand from destroying;  
He has made the rampart and wall lament.  
&emsp;They languish together.

<sup>9</sup> Her gates have sunk into the ground.  
&emsp;He has destroyed and broken her bars.  
Her king and her princes are among the nations where the law is not.  
&emsp;Yes, her prophets find no vision from the LORD.

<sup>10</sup> The elders of the daughter of Zion sit on the ground.  
&emsp;They keep silence.  
They have cast up dust on their heads.  
&emsp;They have clothed themselves with sackcloth.  
&emsp;The virgins of Jerusalem hang down their heads to the ground.

<sup>11</sup> My eyes fail with tears.  
&emsp;My heart is troubled.  
My bile is poured on the earth,  
&emsp;because of the destruction of the daughter of my people,  
&emsp;because the young children and the infants swoon in the streets of the city.

<sup>12</sup> They ask their mothers,  
&emsp;“Where is grain and wine?”  
&emsp;when they swoon as the wounded in the streets of the city,  
&emsp;when their soul is poured out into their mothers’ bosom.

<sup>13</sup> What shall I testify to you?  
&emsp;What shall I liken to you, daughter of Jerusalem?  
What shall I compare to you,  
&emsp;that I may comfort you, virgin daughter of Zion?  
For your breach is as big as the sea.  
&emsp;Who can heal you?

<sup>14</sup> Your prophets have seen false and foolish visions for you.  
&emsp;They have not uncovered your iniquity,  
&emsp;to reverse your captivity,  
&emsp;but have seen for you false revelations and causes of banishment.

<sup>15</sup> All that pass by clap their hands at you.  
&emsp;They hiss and wag their head at the daughter of Jerusalem, saying,  
“Is this the city that men called ‘The perfection of beauty,  
&emsp;the joy of the whole earth’?”

<sup>16</sup> All your enemies have opened their mouth wide against you.  
&emsp;They hiss and gnash their teeth.  
&emsp;They say, “We have swallowed her up.  
Certainly this is the day that we looked for.  
&emsp;We have found it.  
&emsp;We have seen it.”

<sup>17</sup> The LORD has done that which he planned.  
&emsp;He has fulfilled his word that he commanded in the days of old.  
He has thrown down,  
&emsp;and has not pitied.  
He has caused the enemy to rejoice over you.  
&emsp;He has exalted the horn of your adversaries.

<sup>18</sup> Their heart cried to the Lord.  
&emsp;O wall of the daughter of Zion,  
&emsp;let tears run down like a river day and night.  
Give yourself no relief.  
&emsp;Don’t let your eyes rest.

<sup>19</sup> Arise, cry out in the night,  
&emsp;at the beginning of the watches!  
Pour out your heart like water before the face of the Lord.  
&emsp;Lift up your hands toward him for the life of your young children,  
&emsp;who faint for hunger at the head of every street.

<sup>20</sup> “Look, LORD, and see to whom you have done thus!  
&emsp;Should the women eat their offspring,  
&emsp;the children that they held and bounced on their knees?  
&emsp;Should the priest and the prophet be killed in the sanctuary of the Lord?

<sup>21</sup> “The youth and the old man lie on the ground in the streets.  
&emsp;My virgins and my young men have fallen by the sword.  
You have killed them in the day of your anger.  
&emsp;You have slaughtered, and not pitied.

<sup>22</sup> “You have called, as in the day of a solemn assembly, my terrors on every side.  
&emsp;There was no one that escaped or remained in the day of the LORD’s anger.  
&emsp;My enemy has consumed those whom I have cared for and brought up.

## Lamentations 3

<sup>1</sup> I am the man who has seen affliction  
&emsp;by the rod of his wrath.  
<sup>2</sup> He has led me and caused me to walk in darkness,  
&emsp;and not in light.  
<sup>3</sup> Surely he turns his hand against me  
&emsp;again and again all day long.

<sup>4</sup> He has made my flesh and my skin old.  
&emsp;He has broken my bones.  
<sup>5</sup> He has built against me,  
&emsp;and surrounded me with bitterness and hardship.  
<sup>6</sup> He has made me dwell in dark places,  
&emsp;as those who have been long dead.

<sup>7</sup> He has walled me about, so that I can’t go out.  
&emsp;He has made my chain heavy.  
<sup>8</sup> Yes, when I cry, and call for help,  
&emsp;he shuts out my prayer.  
<sup>9</sup> He has walled up my ways with cut stone.  
&emsp;He has made my paths crooked.

<sup>10</sup> He is to me as a bear lying in wait,  
&emsp;as a lion in hiding.  
<sup>11</sup> He has turned away my path,  
&emsp;and pulled me in pieces.  
&emsp;He has made me desolate.  
<sup>12</sup> He has bent his bow,  
&emsp;and set me as a mark for the arrow.

<sup>13</sup> He has caused the shafts of his quiver to enter into my kidneys.  
&emsp;<sup>14</sup> I have become a derision to all my people,  
&emsp;and their song all day long.  
<sup>15</sup> He has filled me with bitterness.  
&emsp;He has stuffed me with wormwood.

<sup>16</sup> He has also broken my teeth with gravel.  
&emsp;He has covered me with ashes.  
<sup>17</sup> You have removed my soul far away from peace.  
&emsp;I forgot prosperity.  
<sup>18</sup> I said, “My strength has perished,  
&emsp;along with my expectation from the LORD.”

<sup>19</sup> Remember my affliction and my misery,  
&emsp;the wormwood and the bitterness.  
<sup>20</sup> My soul still remembers them,  
&emsp;and is bowed down within me.  
<sup>21</sup> This I recall to my mind;  
&emsp;therefore I have hope.

<sup>22</sup> It is because of the LORD’s loving kindnesses that we are not consumed,  
&emsp;because his mercies don’t fail.  
<sup>23</sup> They are new every morning.  
&emsp;Great is your faithfulness.  
<sup>24</sup> “The LORD is my portion,” says my soul.  
&emsp;“Therefore I will hope in him.”

<sup>25</sup> The LORD is good to those who wait for him,  
&emsp;to the soul who seeks him.  
<sup>26</sup> It is good that a man should hope  
&emsp;and quietly wait for the salvation of the LORD.  
&emsp;<sup>27</sup> It is good for a man that he bear the yoke in his youth.

<sup>28</sup> Let him sit alone and keep silence,  
&emsp;because he has laid it on him.  
<sup>29</sup> Let him put his mouth in the dust,  
&emsp;if it is so that there may be hope.  
<sup>30</sup> Let him give his cheek to him who strikes him.  
&emsp;Let him be filled full of reproach.

<sup>31</sup> For the Lord will not cast off forever.  
&emsp;<sup>32</sup> For though he causes grief,  
&emsp;yet he will have compassion according to the multitude of his loving kindnesses.  
<sup>33</sup> For he does not afflict willingly,  
&emsp;nor grieve the children of men.

<sup>34</sup> To crush under foot all the prisoners of the earth,  
&emsp;<sup>35</sup> to turn away the right of a man before the face of the Most High,  
&emsp;<sup>36</sup> to subvert a man in his cause, the Lord doesn’t approve.

<sup>37</sup> Who is he who says, and it comes to pass,  
&emsp;when the Lord doesn’t command it?  
<sup>38</sup> Doesn’t evil and good come out of the mouth of the Most High?  
&emsp;<sup>39</sup> Why should a living man complain,  
&emsp;a man for the punishment of his sins?

<sup>40</sup> Let us search and try our ways,  
&emsp;and turn again to the LORD.  
<sup>41</sup> Let’s lift up our heart with our hands to God[^3] in the heavens.  
&emsp;<sup>42</sup> “We have transgressed and have rebelled.  
&emsp;You have not pardoned.

<sup>43</sup> “You have covered us with anger and pursued us.  
&emsp;You have killed.  
&emsp;You have not pitied.  
<sup>44</sup> You have covered yourself with a cloud,  
&emsp;so that no prayer can pass through.  
<sup>45</sup> You have made us an off-scouring and refuse  
&emsp;in the middle of the peoples.

<sup>46</sup> “All our enemies have opened their mouth wide against us.  
&emsp;<sup>47</sup> Terror and the pit have come on us,  
&emsp;devastation and destruction.”

<sup>48</sup> My eye runs down with streams of water,  
&emsp;for the destruction of the daughter of my people.  
<sup>49</sup> My eye pours down  
&emsp;and doesn’t cease,  
&emsp;without any intermission,  
<sup>50</sup> until the LORD looks down,  
&emsp;and sees from heaven.  
<sup>51</sup> My eye affects my soul,  
&emsp;because of all the daughters of my city.

<sup>52</sup> They have chased me relentlessly like a bird,  
&emsp;those who are my enemies without cause.  
<sup>53</sup> They have cut off my life in the dungeon,  
&emsp;and have cast a stone on me.  
<sup>54</sup> Waters flowed over my head.  
&emsp;I said, “I am cut off.”

<sup>55</sup> I called on your name, LORD,  
&emsp;out of the lowest dungeon.  
<sup>56</sup> You heard my voice:  
&emsp;“Don’t hide your ear from my sighing,  
&emsp;and my cry.”

<sup>57</sup> You came near in the day that I called on you.  
&emsp;You said, “Don’t be afraid.”

<sup>58</sup> Lord, you have pleaded the causes of my soul.  
&emsp;You have redeemed my life.  
<sup>59</sup> LORD, you have seen my wrong.  
&emsp;Judge my cause.  
<sup>60</sup> You have seen all their vengeance  
&emsp;and all their plans against me.

<sup>61</sup> You have heard their reproach, LORD,  
&emsp;and all their plans against me,  
<sup>62</sup> the lips of those that rose up against me,  
&emsp;and their plots against me all day long.  
<sup>63</sup> You see their sitting down and their rising up.  
&emsp;I am their song.

<sup>64</sup> You will pay them back, LORD,  
&emsp;according to the work of their hands.  
<sup>65</sup> You will give them hardness of heart,  
&emsp;your curse to them.  
<sup>66</sup> You will pursue them in anger,  
&emsp;and destroy them from under the heavens of the LORD.

## Lamentations 4

<sup>1</sup> How the gold has become dim!  
&emsp;The most pure gold has changed!  
The stones of the sanctuary are poured out  
&emsp;at the head of every street.

<sup>2</sup> The precious sons of Zion,  
&emsp;comparable to fine gold,  
how they are esteemed as earthen pitchers,  
&emsp;the work of the hands of the potter!

<sup>3</sup> Even the jackals offer their breast.  
&emsp;They nurse their young ones.  
But the daughter of my people has become cruel,  
&emsp;like the ostriches in the wilderness.

<sup>4</sup> The tongue of the nursing child clings to the roof of his mouth for thirst.  
&emsp;The young children ask for bread,  
&emsp;and no one breaks it for them.

<sup>5</sup> Those who ate delicacies are desolate in the streets.  
&emsp;Those who were brought up in purple embrace dunghills.

<sup>6</sup> For the iniquity of the daughter of my people is greater than the sin of Sodom,  
&emsp;which was overthrown as in a moment.  
&emsp;No hands were laid on her.

<sup>7</sup> Her nobles were purer than snow.  
&emsp;They were whiter than milk.  
They were more ruddy in body than rubies.  
&emsp;Their polishing was like sapphire.

<sup>8</sup> Their appearance is blacker than a coal.  
&emsp;They are not known in the streets.  
Their skin clings to their bones.  
&emsp;It is withered.  
&emsp;It has become like wood.

<sup>9</sup> Those who are killed with the sword are better than those who are killed with hunger;  
&emsp;for these pine away, stricken through,  
&emsp;for lack of the fruits of the field.

<sup>10</sup> The hands of the pitiful women have boiled their own children.  
&emsp;They were their food in the destruction of the daughter of my people.

<sup>11</sup> The LORD has accomplished his wrath.  
&emsp;He has poured out his fierce anger.  
He has kindled a fire in Zion,  
&emsp;which has devoured its foundations.

<sup>12</sup> The kings of the earth didn’t believe,  
&emsp;neither did all the inhabitants of the world,  
&emsp;that the adversary and the enemy would enter into the gates of Jerusalem.

<sup>13</sup> It is because of the sins of her prophets  
&emsp;and the iniquities of her priests,  
&emsp;that have shed the blood of the just in the middle of her.

<sup>14</sup> They wander as blind men in the streets.  
&emsp;They are polluted with blood,  
&emsp;So that men can’t touch their garments.

<sup>15</sup> “Go away!” they cried to them.  
&emsp;“Unclean! Go away! Go away! Don’t touch!  
When they fled away and wandered, men said among the nations,  
&emsp;“They can’t live here any more.”

<sup>16</sup> The LORD’s anger has scattered them.  
&emsp;He will not pay attention to them any more.  
They didn’t respect the persons of the priests.  
&emsp;They didn’t favor the elders.

<sup>17</sup> Our eyes still fail,  
&emsp;looking in vain for our help.  
&emsp;In our watching we have watched for a nation that could not save.

<sup>18</sup> They hunt our steps,  
&emsp;so that we can’t go in our streets.  
Our end is near.  
&emsp;Our days are fulfilled,  
&emsp;for our end has come.

<sup>19</sup> Our pursuers were swifter than the eagles of the sky.  
&emsp;They chased us on the mountains.  
&emsp;They set an ambush for us in the wilderness.

<sup>20</sup> The breath of our nostrils,  
&emsp;the anointed of the LORD,  
&emsp;was taken in their pits;  
of whom we said,  
&emsp;under his shadow we will live among the nations.

<sup>21</sup> Rejoice and be glad, daughter of Edom,  
&emsp;who dwells in the land of Uz.  
The cup will pass through to you also.  
&emsp;You will be drunken,  
&emsp;and will make yourself naked.

<sup>22</sup> The punishment of your iniquity is accomplished, daughter of Zion.  
&emsp;He will no more carry you away into captivity.  
He will visit your iniquity, daughter of Edom.  
&emsp;He will uncover your sins.

## Lamentations 5

<sup>1</sup> Remember, LORD, what has come on us.  
&emsp;Look, and see our reproach.  
<sup>2</sup> Our inheritance has been turned over to strangers,  
&emsp;our houses to aliens.  
<sup>3</sup> We are orphans and fatherless.  
&emsp;Our mothers are as widows.  
<sup>4</sup> We must pay for water to drink.  
&emsp;Our wood is sold to us.  
<sup>5</sup> Our pursuers are on our necks.  
&emsp;We are weary, and have no rest.  
<sup>6</sup> We have given our hands to the Egyptians,  
&emsp;and to the Assyrians, to be satisfied with bread.  
<sup>7</sup> Our fathers sinned, and are no more.  
&emsp;We have borne their iniquities.  
<sup>8</sup> Servants rule over us.  
&emsp;There is no one to deliver us out of their hand.  
<sup>9</sup> We get our bread at the peril of our lives,  
&emsp;because of the sword in the wilderness.  
<sup>10</sup> Our skin is black like an oven,  
&emsp;because of the burning heat of famine.  
<sup>11</sup> They ravished the women in Zion,  
&emsp;the virgins in the cities of Judah.  
<sup>12</sup> Princes were hanged up by their hands.  
&emsp;The faces of elders were not honored.  
<sup>13</sup> The young men carry millstones.  
&emsp;The children stumbled under loads of wood.  
<sup>14</sup> The elders have ceased from the gate,  
&emsp;and the young men from their music.  
<sup>15</sup> The joy of our heart has ceased.  
&emsp;Our dance is turned into mourning.  
<sup>16</sup> The crown has fallen from our head.  
&emsp;Woe to us, for we have sinned!  
<sup>17</sup> For this our heart is faint.  
&emsp;For these things our eyes are dim:  
<sup>18</sup> for the mountain of Zion, which is desolate.  
&emsp;The foxes walk on it.

<sup>19</sup> You, LORD, remain forever.  
&emsp;Your throne is from generation to generation.  
<sup>20</sup> Why do you forget us forever,  
&emsp;and forsake us for so long a time?  
<sup>21</sup> Turn us to yourself, LORD, and we will be turned.  
&emsp;Renew our days as of old.  
<sup>22</sup> But you have utterly rejected us.  
&emsp;You are very angry against us.

[^1]: **1:5** When rendered in ALL CAPITAL LETTERS, “LORD” or “GOD” is the translation of God’s Proper Name (Hebrew “יהוה”, usually pronounced Yahweh).
[^2]: **1:14** The word translated “Lord” is “Adonai.”
[^3]: **3:41** The Hebrew word rendered “God” is “אֱלֹהִ֑ים” (Elohim).
