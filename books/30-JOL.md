# The Book of Joel

*[World English Bible (Catholic)](README.md) — Text version: signed by eBible.org 2026-07-24 (pulled 2026-07-26).*

## Joel 1

<sup>1</sup> The LORD’s[^1] word that came to Joel, the son of Pethuel.

<sup>2</sup> Hear this, you elders,  
&emsp;and listen, all you inhabitants of the land!  
Has this ever happened in your days,  
&emsp;or in the days of your fathers?  
<sup>3</sup> Tell your children about it,  
&emsp;and have your children tell their children,  
&emsp;and their children, another generation.  
<sup>4</sup> What the swarming locust has left, the great locust has eaten.  
&emsp;What the great locust has left, the grasshopper has eaten.  
&emsp;What the grasshopper has left, the caterpillar has eaten.  
<sup>5</sup> Wake up, you drunkards, and weep!  
&emsp;Wail, all you drinkers of wine, because of the sweet wine,  
&emsp;for it is cut off from your mouth.  
<sup>6</sup> For a nation has come up on my land, strong, and without number.  
&emsp;His teeth are the teeth of a lion,  
&emsp;and he has the fangs of a lioness.  
<sup>7</sup> He has laid my vine waste,  
&emsp;and stripped my fig tree.  
&emsp;He has stripped its bark, and thrown it away.  
&emsp;Its branches are made white.  
<sup>8</sup> Mourn like a virgin dressed in sackcloth  
&emsp;for the husband of her youth!  
<sup>9</sup> The meal offering and the drink offering are cut off from the LORD’s house.  
&emsp;The priests, the LORD’s ministers, mourn.  
<sup>10</sup> The field is laid waste.  
&emsp;The land mourns, for the grain is destroyed,  
&emsp;The new wine has dried up,  
&emsp;and the oil languishes.  
<sup>11</sup> Be confounded, you farmers!  
&emsp;Wail, you vineyard keepers,  
&emsp;for the wheat and for the barley;  
&emsp;for the harvest of the field has perished.  
<sup>12</sup> The vine has dried up, and the fig tree withered—  
&emsp;the pomegranate tree, the palm tree also, and the apple tree,  
&emsp;even all of the trees of the field are withered;  
&emsp;for joy has withered away from the sons of men.  
<sup>13</sup> Put on sackcloth and mourn, you priests!  
&emsp;Wail, you ministers of the altar.  
Come, lie all night in sackcloth, you ministers of my God,[^2]  
&emsp;for the meal offering and the drink offering are withheld from your God’s house.  
<sup>14</sup> Sanctify a fast.  
&emsp;Call a solemn assembly.  
&emsp;Gather the elders and all the inhabitants of the land to the house of the LORD, your God,  
&emsp;and cry to the LORD.  
<sup>15</sup> Alas for the day!  
&emsp;For the day of the LORD is at hand,  
&emsp;and it will come as destruction from the Almighty.  
<sup>16</sup> Isn’t the food cut off before our eyes,  
&emsp;joy and gladness from the house of our God?  
<sup>17</sup> The seeds rot under their clods.  
&emsp;The granaries are laid desolate.  
&emsp;The barns are broken down, for the grain has withered.  
<sup>18</sup> How the animals groan!  
&emsp;The herds of livestock are perplexed, because they have no pasture.  
&emsp;Yes, the flocks of sheep are made desolate.  
<sup>19</sup> LORD, I cry to you,  
&emsp;for the fire has devoured the pastures of the wilderness,  
&emsp;and the flame has burned all the trees of the field.  
<sup>20</sup> Yes, the animals of the field pant to you,  
&emsp;for the water brooks have dried up,  
&emsp;and the fire has devoured the pastures of the wilderness.

## Joel 2

<sup>1</sup> Blow the trumpet in Zion,  
&emsp;and sound an alarm in my holy mountain!  
Let all the inhabitants of the land tremble,  
&emsp;for the day of the LORD comes,  
&emsp;for it is close at hand:  
<sup>2</sup> A day of darkness and gloominess,  
&emsp;a day of clouds and thick darkness.  
As the dawn spreading on the mountains,  
&emsp;a great and strong people;  
&emsp;there has never been the like,  
&emsp;neither will there be any more after them,  
&emsp;even to the years of many generations.  
<sup>3</sup> A fire devours before them,  
&emsp;and behind them, a flame burns.  
The land is as the garden of Eden before them,  
&emsp;and behind them, a desolate wilderness.  
Yes, and no one has escaped them.  
<sup>4</sup> Their appearance is as the appearance of horses,  
&emsp;and they run as horsemen.  
<sup>5</sup> Like the noise of chariots on the tops of the mountains, they leap,  
&emsp;like the noise of a flame of fire that devours the stubble,  
&emsp;like a strong people set in battle array.  
<sup>6</sup> At their presence the peoples are in anguish.  
&emsp;All faces have grown pale.  
<sup>7</sup> They run like mighty men.  
&emsp;They climb the wall like warriors.  
&emsp;They each march in his line, and they don’t swerve off course.  
<sup>8</sup> One doesn’t jostle another.  
&emsp;They each march in their own path.  
&emsp;They burst through the defenses  
&emsp;and don’t break ranks.  
<sup>9</sup> They rush on the city.  
&emsp;They run on the wall.  
&emsp;They climb up into the houses.  
&emsp;They enter in at the windows like thieves.  
<sup>10</sup> The earth quakes before them.  
&emsp;The heavens tremble.  
&emsp;The sun and the moon are darkened,  
&emsp;and the stars withdraw their shining.  
<sup>11</sup> The LORD thunders his voice before his army,  
&emsp;for his forces are very great;  
&emsp;for he is strong who obeys his command;  
&emsp;for the day of the LORD is great and very awesome,  
&emsp;and who can endure it?  
<sup>12</sup> “Yet even now,” says the LORD, “turn to me with all your heart,  
&emsp;and with fasting, and with weeping, and with mourning.”  
<sup>13</sup> Tear your heart and not your garments,  
&emsp;and turn to the LORD, your God;  
&emsp;for he is gracious and merciful,  
&emsp;slow to anger, and abundant in loving kindness,  
&emsp;and relents from sending calamity.  
<sup>14</sup> Who knows? He may turn and relent,  
&emsp;and leave a blessing behind him,  
&emsp;even a meal offering and a drink offering to the LORD, your God.  
<sup>15</sup> Blow the trumpet in Zion!  
&emsp;Sanctify a fast.  
&emsp;Call a solemn assembly.  
<sup>16</sup> Gather the people.  
&emsp;Sanctify the assembly.  
&emsp;Assemble the elders.  
&emsp;Gather the children, and those who nurse from breasts.  
Let the bridegroom go out of his room,  
&emsp;and the bride out of her chamber.  
<sup>17</sup> Let the priests, the ministers of the LORD, weep between the porch and the altar,  
&emsp;and let them say, “Spare your people, LORD,  
&emsp;and don’t give your heritage to reproach,  
&emsp;that the nations should rule over them.  
Why should they say among the peoples,  
&emsp;‘Where is their God?’”  
<sup>18</sup> Then the LORD was jealous for his land,  
&emsp;and had pity on his people.  
<sup>19</sup> The LORD answered his people,  
&emsp;“Behold,[^3] I will send you grain, new wine, and oil,  
&emsp;and you will be satisfied with them;  
&emsp;and I will no more make you a reproach among the nations.  
<sup>20</sup> But I will remove the northern army far away from you,  
&emsp;and will drive it into a barren and desolate land,  
&emsp;its front into the eastern sea,  
&emsp;and its back into the western sea;  
&emsp;and its stench will come up,  
&emsp;and its bad smell will rise.”  
Surely he has done great things.  
<sup>21</sup> Land, don’t be afraid.  
&emsp;Be glad and rejoice, for the LORD has done great things.  
<sup>22</sup> Don’t be afraid, you animals of the field;  
&emsp;for the pastures of the wilderness spring up,  
&emsp;for the tree bears its fruit.  
&emsp;The fig tree and the vine yield their strength.

<sup>23</sup> “Be glad then, you children of Zion,  
&emsp;and rejoice in the LORD, your God;  
&emsp;for he gives you the early rain in just measure,  
&emsp;and he causes the rain to come down for you,  
&emsp;the early rain and the latter rain, as before.  
<sup>24</sup> The threshing floors will be full of wheat,  
&emsp;and the vats will overflow with new wine and oil.  
<sup>25</sup> I will restore to you the years that the swarming locust has eaten,  
&emsp;the great locust, the grasshopper, and the caterpillar,  
&emsp;my great army, which I sent among you.  
<sup>26</sup> You will have plenty to eat and be satisfied,  
&emsp;and will praise the name of the LORD, your God,  
&emsp;who has dealt wondrously with you;  
&emsp;and my people will never again be disappointed.  
<sup>27</sup> You will know that I am among Israel,  
&emsp;and that I am the LORD, your God, and there is no one else;  
&emsp;and my people will never again be disappointed.

<sup>28</sup> “It will happen afterward, that I will pour out my Spirit on all flesh;  
&emsp;and your sons and your daughters will prophesy.  
&emsp;Your old men will dream dreams.  
&emsp;Your young men will see visions.  
<sup>29</sup> And also on the servants and on the handmaids in those days,  
&emsp;I will pour out my Spirit.  
<sup>30</sup> I will show wonders in the heavens and on the earth:  
&emsp;blood, fire, and pillars of smoke.  
<sup>31</sup> The sun will be turned into darkness,  
&emsp;and the moon into blood,  
&emsp;before the great and terrible day of the LORD comes.  
<sup>32</sup> It will happen that whoever will call on the LORD’s name shall be saved;  
&emsp;for in Mount Zion and in Jerusalem there will be those who escape,  
&emsp;as the LORD has said,  
&emsp;and among the remnant, those whom the LORD calls.

## Joel 3

<sup>1</sup> “For, behold, in those days,  
&emsp;and in that time,  
&emsp;when I restore the fortunes of Judah and Jerusalem,  
<sup>2</sup> I will gather all nations,  
&emsp;and will bring them down into the valley of Jehoshaphat;  
&emsp;and I will execute judgment on them there for my people,  
&emsp;and for my heritage, Israel, whom they have scattered among the nations.  
&emsp;They have divided my land,  
&emsp;<sup>3</sup> and have cast lots for my people,  
&emsp;and have given a boy for a prostitute,  
&emsp;and sold a girl for wine, that they may drink.

<sup>4</sup> “Yes, and what are you to me, Tyre and Sidon,  
&emsp;and all the regions of Philistia?  
Will you repay me?  
&emsp;And if you repay me,  
&emsp;I will swiftly and speedily return your repayment on your own head.  
<sup>5</sup> Because you have taken my silver and my gold,  
&emsp;and have carried my finest treasures into your temples,  
&emsp;<sup>6</sup> and have sold the children of Judah and the children of Jerusalem to the sons of the Greeks,  
&emsp;that you may remove them far from their border.  
<sup>7</sup> Behold, I will stir them up out of the place where you have sold them,  
&emsp;and will return your repayment on your own head;  
<sup>8</sup> and I will sell your sons and your daughters into the hands of the children of Judah,  
&emsp;and they will sell them to the men of Sheba,  
&emsp;to a faraway nation,  
&emsp;for the LORD has spoken it.”

<sup>9</sup> Proclaim this among the nations:  
&emsp;“Prepare for war!  
&emsp;Stir up the mighty men.  
Let all the warriors draw near.  
&emsp;Let them come up. <sup>10</sup> Beat your plowshares into swords,  
&emsp;and your pruning hooks into spears.  
&emsp;Let the weak say, ‘I am strong.’  
<sup>11</sup> Hurry and come, all you surrounding nations,  
&emsp;and gather yourselves together.”  
Cause your mighty ones to come down there, LORD.  
<sup>12</sup> “Let the nations arouse themselves,  
&emsp;and come up to the valley of Jehoshaphat;  
&emsp;for there I will sit to judge all the surrounding nations.  
<sup>13</sup> Put in the sickle;  
&emsp;for the harvest is ripe.  
&emsp;Come, tread, for the wine press is full,  
&emsp;the vats overflow, for their wickedness is great.”  
<sup>14</sup> Multitudes, multitudes in the valley of decision!  
&emsp;For the day of the LORD is near in the valley of decision.  
<sup>15</sup> The sun and the moon are darkened,  
&emsp;and the stars withdraw their shining.  
<sup>16</sup> The LORD will roar from Zion,  
&emsp;and thunder from Jerusalem;  
&emsp;and the heavens and the earth will shake;  
&emsp;but the LORD will be a refuge to his people,  
&emsp;and a stronghold to the children of Israel.  
<sup>17</sup> “So you will know that I am the LORD, your God,  
&emsp;dwelling in Zion, my holy mountain.  
Then Jerusalem will be holy,  
&emsp;and no strangers will pass through her any more.  
<sup>18</sup> It will happen in that day,  
&emsp;that the mountains will drop down sweet wine,  
&emsp;the hills will flow with milk,  
&emsp;all the brooks of Judah will flow with waters;  
&emsp;and a fountain will flow out from the LORD’s house,  
&emsp;and will water the valley of Shittim.  
<sup>19</sup> Egypt will be a desolation  
&emsp;and Edom will be a desolate wilderness,  
&emsp;for the violence done to the children of Judah,  
&emsp;because they have shed innocent blood in their land.  
<sup>20</sup> But Judah will be inhabited forever,  
&emsp;and Jerusalem from generation to generation.  
<sup>21</sup> I will cleanse their blood  
&emsp;that I have not cleansed,  
&emsp;for the LORD dwells in Zion.”

[^1]: **1:1** When rendered in ALL CAPITAL LETTERS, “LORD” or “GOD” is the translation of God’s Proper Name (Hebrew “יהוה”, usually pronounced Yahweh).
[^2]: **1:13** The Hebrew word rendered “God” is “אֱלֹהִ֑ים” (Elohim).
[^3]: **2:19** “Behold”, from “הִנֵּה”, means look at, take notice, observe, see, or gaze at. It is often used as an interjection.
